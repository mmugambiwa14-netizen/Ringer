Privacy Policy

Ringer

Ringer does not collect your personal information. Everything the app stores stays on your device.

Last updated: 18 August 2026

What stays on your phone

Ringer is a pass-and-play party game. It has no accounts, no servers and no network features. The following is written to your device only, and never leaves it:

  • Player names you type, and the icons assigned to them
  • Scores and the current round
  • Your settings — sound, haptics, reveal style, word packs

Deleting the app deletes all of it. There is no copy anywhere else.

Anonymous usage statistics — off by default

Settings contains an Anonymous stats switch. It is off unless you turn it on, and the game behaves identically either way.

With it on, the app records counts such as how many rounds a group played, which game mode was chosen and how many players were at the table. It does not record:

  • Player names, or any text you type
  • The words the game showed you
  • Any device identifier, advertising identifier or IP-based profile
  • Anything that could identify you or your phone

These counts exist for one reason: to tell us whether the word lists are any good. If groups stop after one round, the words need work.

No advertising

Ringer contains no advertising and no advertising SDKs. It does not track you across other apps or websites, and there is nothing to opt out of.

Children

Ringer is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age. The 18+ word pack is hidden until an adult switches it on in settings.

Purchases

If you buy anything, the transaction is handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

Your rights

Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete on your behalf. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes everything Ringer has stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date, and any change that affects what is collected will be surfaced in the app first.
